Back in late 2020 during my early stages of learning VBA (Visual Basic for Applications), I took on a side project called Cube Runner to further develop my skills. Cube Runner is a game created using VBA and user forms within Microsoft Excel. This project allowed me to apply my knowledge, challenge myself, and showcase my progress. Here is an overview of the project.

[![Cube Runner Game Demo](/img/demos/QubeRunner_Demo.gif)](/img/demos/QubeRunner_Demo.gif)

### The Game Concept

Cube Runner introduces a simple gameplay concept. Players control a pink "cubeMan" character with the objective of collecting cubes that spawn at random intervals in various locations on the screen. Additionally, there are enemy blue boxes that chase the player, and prolonged contact with them will result in a game over. The game offers endless level progression, allowing players to play for the highscore indefinitely.
