### Course Description

DATA 101 is an introduction to data science: cleaning and organizing real datasets, building weighted models and rankings, comparing groups statistically, and communicating findings through written reports and visualizations.

### Projects

<details class="course-section">
<summary>College Rankings: A Personal View</summary>
<p>Built a personalized college-ranking algorithm, weighting cost, retention rate, and diversity metrics to match the needs of a specific hypothetical student.</p>
<p><a href="/demos/DATA101/01-college-rankings.pdf">View Report</a></p>
</details>

<details class="course-section">
<summary>COVID Vaccination Trends: Germany, France & the US</summary>
<p>Analyzed COVID-19 vaccination and booster trends in Germany, France, and the United States using public OWID data, comparing adoption rates per capita across all three countries.</p>
<p><a href="/demos/DATA101/02-covid-vaccination-trends.pdf">View Report</a></p>
</details>

<details class="course-section">
<summary>Weapon Preference in Homicides: A Gendered Perspective</summary>
<p>Examined FBI crime data from 1980&ndash;2014 to explore how perpetrator and victim sex relate to weapon choice in homicides.</p>

</details>
